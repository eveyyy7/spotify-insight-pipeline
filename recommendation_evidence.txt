Spotify Insight Pipeline - Recommendation Evidence

PRIMARY RECOMMENDATION

Prioritize reducing free-tier friction caused by Premium gating of core playback controls, especially:
- song selection
- queue management
- repeat
- skip / previous
- seeking within tracks

THEME RANKING

1. free_tier_premium_gating
   - Priority score: 2474
   - Complaint count: 833
   - Mean severity: 2.970

2. playback_reliability
   - Priority score: 1221
   - Complaint count: 345
   - Mean severity: 3.539

3. ads_experience
   - Priority score: 1191
   - Complaint count: 520
   - Mean severity: 2.290

4. product_regression_ui
   - Priority score: 973
   - Complaint count: 351
   - Mean severity: 2.772

WHY #1 WINS

Free-tier Premium gating is not the most severe issue per complaint,
but it combines high complaint volume with meaningful severity.

Its cumulative priority score (2474) is approximately twice that of
playback reliability (1221).

Ads generate more complaints than several other issues, but their
mean severity is substantially lower (2.29), producing a lower
priority score (1191).

REPRESENTATIVE USER EVIDENCE

Quote 1:

COMPARISON

Playback reliability:
- Higher mean severity (3.539)
- Lower affected complaint volume (345)
- Priority score 1221

Ads experience:
- Higher complaint volume than many individual sub-issues (520)
- Lower mean severity (2.290)
- Priority score 1191

Therefore, Premium gating creates the largest cumulative complaint
burden among the actionable themes in the 10K analysis.
